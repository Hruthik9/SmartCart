How to run it

Tap the phone like a real app. Every button works. Things drawn in blue dashed lines are prototype controls that stand in for the real world, for example choosing which product the camera is pointed at, or what the shopper says out loud. Sounds are written in the status pill (♪) instead of played.

    Usage scenarios to try

    1. Priya, weekly Walmart run on a $20 budget
    1. Start a trip at Walmart, set the budget to $20.
    2. Scan Cheerios, Frosted Flakes, Almond Milk and Paper Towels. They land in Snaps, not the cart.
    3. Open Snaps and tap Add all. The over-budget alert pops up.
    4. Tap See swaps, swap the cereals and paper towels, and finish under budget.
    2. Marcus, checking allergens for his son
    1. Scan Almond Milk and Chocolate Cookies and read the allergen chips on each card.
    2. Tap the mic and pick “Is this safe for a nut allergy?”
    3. Scan Strawberry Yogurt, add it, then take the healthier swap on the Swaps tab.
    3. Dana, choosing a phone charger at Target
    1. Start a trip at Target. Scan the Anker charger, then the Apple charger.
    2. Open Snaps, tick both, tap Compare.
    3. Pick one and add it to the cart. The other stays in Snaps.
